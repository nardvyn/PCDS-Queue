# Problem Statement

Manual queueing in service offices can produce long physical lines, unclear
service order, repeated inquiries, and difficulty tracking whether a customer
was served, absent, or completed. Staff members also need a consistent way to
identify their assigned window and manage queue activity across departments.

Without a centralized queue record, administrators cannot easily monitor live
waiting counts, window usage, service outcomes, or historical queue activity.
Students may need to remain in a line simply to learn whether their number is
near service.

The PCDS Queue Management System addresses these issues by centralizing queue
number generation and service actions. It provides a controlled order for each
department, presents current calls on the TV Display, lets staff operate only
their active shift window, and records queue events for history and reporting.
