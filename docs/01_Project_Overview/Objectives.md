# Objectives

## General objective

To develop a centralized queue management system that improves the delivery,
monitoring, and visibility of services at PCDS.

## Specific objectives

1. Generate unique daily queue numbers for each active department.
2. Allow students to join a queue through the kiosk or mobile application.
3. Provide secure role-based access for administrators and service staff.
4. Let staff select an available window, start a shift, and process the next
   eligible customer in their department.
5. Support recall, completion, no-show, and end-shift actions with recorded
   queue events.
6. Display live serving and waiting information for each department.
7. Allow administrators to manage departments, service windows, staff,
   settings, QR expiration, and queue-channel availability.
8. Preserve queue history and reports to support operational review.
9. Support local and campus-network deployment without exposing MySQL directly
   to client applications.
